15+ Questions Answered

Why should I choose Kaylir Tech?

We study your business before we build. Unlike agencies that use one-size-fits-all templates, we create custom strategies tailored to your unique business needs. We're based in Tamil Nadu, communicate in Tamil and English, and focus on real, measurable results.

How long does website development take?

A standard business website typically takes 2-4 weeks from start to launch. The timeline depends on the complexity and how quickly we receive content and feedback from you. We'll provide a clear timeline during our free consultation.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Absolutely. We offer ongoing support and maintenance packages to keep your website updated, secure, and performing at its best. We're always just a call or WhatsApp message away for any issues or updates.

Will I receive source code ownership?

Yes, you will have full ownership of the source code once the project is complete. We believe in transparency — your website is your asset, and we provide all files, credentials, and documentation.

Do you offer payment installments?

Yes, we offer flexible milestone-based payment schedules for larger projects. Typically, we work with a 50% advance and 50% on completion structure, and custom plans can be discussed during consultation.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes, we specialise in redesigning and modernising existing websites. We'll preserve your content and SEO equity while giving you a fresh, modern look with better performance and user experience.

Do you provide SEO services?

Yes, all our websites come with basic SEO optimization. We also offer advanced SEO services including keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO, and local SEO for businesses looking to rank higher on Google.

What industries do you work with?

We work with a wide range of industries including restaurants, real estate, healthcare clinics, educational institutions, construction companies, retail businesses, startups, and professional services. Our solutions are tailored to each industry's unique needs.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we are happy to sign Non-Disclosure Agreements to protect your business ideas and confidential information. We take data privacy and confidentiality very seriously.

What if I am not satisfied with the service?

Your satisfaction is our priority. We work closely with you throughout the process and incorporate your feedback at every stage. If something isn't right, we'll make it right. We also offer a risk-free trial for our social media management service.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every website we build is mobile-first and fully responsive. It will look great and perform perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops — which is essential for both user experience and SEO rankings.

Do you provide website hosting?

Yes, we offer reliable hosting solutions with 99.9% uptime. We handle all the technical setup including domain configuration, SSL certificates, and security so you can focus on running your business.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and card payments. For larger projects, we offer a milestone-based payment schedule to make it easier for you.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We'll ensure a smooth transition and provide you with all the assets and access you need. No hidden charges or lock-in contracts.

Do you charge for the initial consultation?

No. The initial consultation is completely free. We'll discuss your business, understand your goals, and suggest a custom plan — with zero obligation. We believe in building trust first.
