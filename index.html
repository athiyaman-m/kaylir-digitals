15+ Questions Answered

What services does Kaylir Digitals offer?

We offer four core services: Website Design & Development (custom-built, mobile-responsive sites), Social Media Design & Management (content strategy, post creation, engagement), Meta Ads Management (targeted Facebook and Instagram advertising with ROI tracking), and AI Integration & Chatbots (smart automation for customer support and lead capture).

How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start at ₹15,000. The final price depends on the complexity and features you need. We provide a detailed quote after understanding your requirements during a free consultation.

Do you work with businesses outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes! While we are based in Tamil Nadu and specialise in the local market, we work with businesses across India. All our services can be delivered remotely through virtual meetings and online collaboration.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website typically takes 2-4 weeks from start to launch. The timeline depends on the complexity and how quickly we receive content and feedback from you.

Do you offer support after the project is complete?

Absolutely. We provide ongoing support and maintenance packages to keep your website updated, secure, and performing at its best. We're always just a call or message away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and card payments. For larger projects, we offer a milestone-based payment schedule.

Is there a minimum commitment for social media management?

We recommend a minimum of 3 months to see meaningful results. However, we offer a 15-day free trial so you can experience our service quality before committing.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We'll ensure a smooth transition and provide you with all the assets and access you need.

Do you charge for the initial consultation?

No. The initial consultation is completely free. We'll discuss your business, understand your goals, and suggest a custom plan — with zero obligation.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every website we build is mobile-first and fully responsive. It will look great and perform perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Do you provide website hosting?

Yes, we offer reliable hosting solutions with 99.9% uptime. We handle all the technical setup so you can focus on running your business.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

Yes. We build websites on easy-to-use platforms that allow you to update content yourself. We also provide basic training and documentation.

Do you handle SEO?

Yes, all our websites come with basic SEO optimisation. We also offer advanced SEO services for businesses looking to rank higher on Google.

What platforms do you use for building websites?

We use modern technologies based on your needs — from custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript for simple sites to React, Next.js, and WordPress for more complex requirements.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, we specialise in redesigning and modernising existing websites. We'll preserve your content while giving you a fresh, modern look with better performance.
